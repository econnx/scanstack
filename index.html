ScanStack

Phone photos of your notebook → one clean PDF, ready for Moodle or Google Classroom. Removes shadows, whitens the paper and keeps the file small. Everything runs in your browser — your pages are never uploaded.

Drop photos here or click to choose JPG or PNG · any number of pages · you can also paste with Ctrl/Cmd+V

Tips

  • Shoot from straight above, in daylight, with the page filling most of the frame.
  • Drag pages to reorder (or use ← →), ⟳ rotates, ✕ removes.
  • "Clean scan" divides out the uneven lighting, so shadows from your phone disappear and pencil stays readable.
  • The final size is shown after export. If your LMS limit is strict, pick "Small" or "Black & white".
